A few details from the review, so the notes match what actually happened, not the original plan.

1. The cutover runs per service, not per database. Orders can move on Tuesday; the ledger waits for the reconciliation window on Wednesday.

2. Each service keeps its old connection string behind a flag for 48 hours. The flag is removed only after the p99 chart is flat for a full business day.

3. The rollback is a flag flip, not a restore. Restoring a snapshot loses the writes made during the cutover, so we do not do that unless the ledger itself is corrupt.
